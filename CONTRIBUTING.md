## AI development disclosure

## Authority and approval

- Everyone can propose issues, ideas, documentation fixes and pull requests; contributions are welcome.
- Only @TheRoyalCaptain decides whether a change enters the official Domyro repository, official release, roadmap or brand.
- An open PR, a favorable review, a CI green check, or other contributors' opinions never amount to official approval.
- Contributors do **not** need permission to maintain compatible forks under AGPLv3. A fork is not an official Domyro release and may not misrepresent itself as such.
- There is no guaranteed acceptance timeline. Maintainer may request changes, close a PR or defer it.

## Before implementing

The owner explicitly approved **limited M1 foundation development on 31 October 2026**. Source proposals may now be submitted, but official merges/releases remain solely at @TheRoyalCaptain's discretion, and untested production deployments or device-control claims are not accepted. See `docs/G0-IMPLEMENTATION-START.md`.

## Implementation workflow after limited G0 foundation approval

1. Discuss major changes in an issue, link the relevant product/architecture decision.
2. Fork and use a topical branch; do not push directly to `main`.
3. Open a PR with problem, scope, tests, security/privacy impact, migration risks and screenshots for UI changes.
4. Await review by @TheRoyalCaptain. Only this owner can grant the official authorization to merge; GitHub rules require owner review from code ownership.
5. Do not add unlicensed or incompatible third-party code, vendor credentials, private data, telemetry or unverifiable dependencies.
6. Updates to security/architecture/permissions require corresponding documentation and tests.
